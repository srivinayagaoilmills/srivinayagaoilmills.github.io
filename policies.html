Return & Refund Policyதிரும்பப்பெறல் & பணத்திருப்பம் கொள்கை

Our oils and flours are food products. For the safety of every customer, we do not accept returns of food items once they are delivered, and we cannot resell an opened or returned bottle. We take great care over every batch, so if something is wrong, we want to hear about it.

When we will help

We will replace the item or refund you, as you prefer, only if one of these is true:

  • the product has a genuine quality problem (for example a spoiled smell or taste, or foreign matter in the product);
  • the parcel arrived damaged or leaking;
  • we sent you a wrong item or a wrong quantity.

What you need to do

  • Tell us within 2 days (48 hours) of delivery by calling or messaging WhatsApp on the numbers below.
  • Send an unboxing video, recorded without a break from the moment you open the sealed parcel, showing the label, the batch details and the problem. Clear photos of the product and the outer box are also needed.
  • Keep the product and packing as they are until we have looked at it. We may ask you to send the item back or may arrange a pickup.

How we decide

We check your video and photos and, where needed, the batch record. We will tell you our decision within 3 working days. If your claim is accepted, we send a replacement at our cost, or refund the amount you paid for the affected item.

How refunds are paid

  • Refunds go back to the same method you paid with (for example the UPI account or bank account you used), usually within 5 to 7 working days of approval.
  • If you paid in cash on delivery, we will refund by UPI or bank transfer to an account you give us.
  • If you used reward points on that order, the points for the refunded item are returned to your account.

What is not covered

  • Change of mind, or not liking the taste, colour or aroma of a natural oil. Natural oils differ a little from batch to batch, and gingelly, castor and neem oils have strong, characteristic smells.
  • Products opened and used, or stored badly after delivery.
  • Claims reported after 2 days of delivery, or without an unboxing video.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel before we pack the order by contacting us. Once an order is packed or on its way, it cannot be cancelled. If you have already paid for a cancelled order, we refund the full amount.

Shipping & Delivery Policyஷிப்பிங் & டெலிவரி கொள்கை

Where we deliver

We deliver across Tamil Nadu. For addresses outside Tamil Nadu, please call or message us first and we will tell you if we can send to you.

Delivery charges

The delivery fee, and the order amount above which delivery is free, are shown in your cart before you place the order. The amount you see at checkout is the amount you pay; we add nothing after that.

How long it takes

  • We confirm your order by phone or message, then pack and dispatch it.
  • Orders within Tamil Nadu usually reach you in 2 to 5 working days after dispatch. Remote areas can take a little longer.
  • Festival seasons, heavy rain and transport strikes can cause delay. We will tell you if that happens.
  • You can follow your order at any time on the Track order section with your order number and mobile number.

When your parcel arrives

  • Please keep your phone reachable on the mobile number you gave, so the delivery person can reach you.
  • Check that the parcel is sealed and not leaking before you accept it. Record an unboxing video when you open it. This protects you if there is a problem (see the Return & Refund Policy).
  • If the delivery fails because the address is wrong or nobody is reachable, we may charge the cost of sending it again.

Payment

You can pay online (UPI, which our team confirms) or pay offline, as shown at checkout. Your order is recorded as soon as you place it, and we confirm payment before dispatch where you have chosen to pay online.

Privacy Policyதனியுரிமைக் கொள்கை

We collect only what we need to take and deliver your order. We never sell your details.

What we collect

  • Account and order details: your name, mobile number, delivery address, pincode, the items you order, and your order history and reward points.
  • Optional details: a referral code, product reviews you choose to post, and any message you send through our enquiry forms.
  • Basic technical data: such as language and theme choices stored on your own device so the site remembers you.

Your password is stored in a protected form, and we cannot read it.

Why we use it

  • to confirm, pack, deliver and track your order, and to call or message you about it;
  • to give you an invoice, reward points and referral benefits;
  • to look into a quality complaint;
  • to keep the site safe and prevent misuse, including checks when someone resets a password.

Who sees it

  • Our own team handling orders, through a protected admin area. New orders are also alerted to our private team group on Telegram, with the details needed to pack and deliver.
  • Our hosting and database service provider, which stores the data for us under its own security terms.
  • Delivery partners, who receive only your name, address and phone number for delivery.
  • Authorities, if the law requires us to share information.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed for your service, accounting and legal requirements. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal details, except records we must legally keep.

Your choices

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by contacting us below.
  • You can stop messages from us by telling us.
  • You can clear the language and theme settings by clearing your browser's site data.

Security

We use access controls so customers can only see their own information. No online system is completely risk-free, so please keep your password private and do not share it with anyone, including our staff.

Children

This site is meant for adults. Please do not place orders on behalf of a child without a parent's knowledge.

Changes

If we change this policy, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.

ஆரோக்கியக் குறிப்பு

எங்கள் எண்ணெய்கள் உணவுப் பொருட்கள், மருந்துகள் அல்ல. பாரம்பரியப் பயன்பாடு பற்றிய தகவல்கள் பொது அறிவிற்காக மட்டுமே; மருத்துவ ஆலோசனை அல்ல. ஒவ்வாமை அல்லது உடல்நலப் பிரச்சினை இருந்தால் மருத்துவரிடம் கேளுங்கள். விளக்கெண்ணெய், வேப்பெண்ணெய் ஆகியவை அவற்றின் பக்கங்களில் கூறப்பட்ட பயன்பாட்டிற்கானவை; சமையலுக்கு அல்ல.

எங்கள் பொறுப்பு

இணையதளம் கிடைக்கும்படியும் துல்லியமாகவும் இருக்க முயல்கிறோம்; ஆனால் பிழைகள் அல்லது தடங்கல்கள் எப்போதும் இருக்காது என்று உறுதியளிக்க முடியாது. சட்டம் அனுமதிக்கும் அளவிற்கு, எந்த ஆர்டருக்குமான எங்கள் பொறுப்பு அதற்கு நீங்கள் செலுத்திய தொகை வரை மட்டுமே.
